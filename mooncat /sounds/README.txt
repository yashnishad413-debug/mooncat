Moon Cat sound effects
======================

Drop these three royalty-free .wav files into this folder, then run ./build.sh again:

  meow.wav   a short cat meow (1-2 seconds)
  purr.wav   a purr that can loop (3-5 seconds)
  chime.wav  a soft chime or bell (1-2 seconds)

No rebuild needed: you can also put the same three files in
  ~/Library/Application Support/MoonCat/sounds/
and she will use them the next time she starts.

Where to find free sounds: freesound.org (filter by CC0), pixabay.com/sound-effects,
mixkit.co/free-sound-effects. 16-bit PCM WAV, 44.1 or 48 kHz, under 500 KB each.
If a file is missing she simply stays quiet.
